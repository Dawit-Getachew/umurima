Rwanda has three cropping seasons. Season A runs from September to February, Season B from February to June or July, and Season C from July to September. Season C is mainly for marshland and irrigated land. In Kinyarwanda a season is igihembwe and the rainy season is imvura.

When to plant maize: plant maize in mid September for Season A, and in late February or March for Season B, in both cases once the rains are steady. When to plant beans: the same two windows. When to plant Irish potato: September for Season A in the highlands.

Season A is the main season. Land preparation happens in August, planting starts with the first reliable rains in mid September, and harvest is from January to February. Maize, beans, soybean and Irish potato are the common Season A crops.

Season B starts with the long rains around late February and March. Harvest is in June and July. Beans, maize, sorghum, vegetables and sweet potato are common. Season B rains are usually heavier, so drainage and erosion control matter more.

Season C runs from July to September in valleys, marshlands and irrigated schemes. Vegetables, beans and maize are grown where water is available. Most hillside rainfed land rests during this period.

Do not plant before the rains are established. Planting on one or two early showers often means the seed germinates and then dries out. Wait until rain has fallen steadily for about a week, and ask your Farmer Promoter what the local advice is for your sector this season.

Rainfall differs by region. The Northern and Western highlands are cooler and wetter and suit Irish potato, wheat and maize. The Eastern Province is warmer and drier and suits maize, beans, cassava, banana and rice in the marshlands. The Bugesera area is drought prone and needs early maturing and drought tolerant varieties.

Climb the calendar backwards when choosing a variety. If a variety needs 120 days and your rains stop in 90 days, the crop will fail. Choose short duration varieties in drought prone sectors and ask RAB or your agro dealer for the maturity period before buying seed.
